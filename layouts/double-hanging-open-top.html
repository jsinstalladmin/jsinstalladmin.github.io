Double hanging with an open top

The same two rods, run clear to the top with no shelf capping them.

The numbers

What this section needs

Upper rodSet from the ceiling, not from a shelf
Lower rod40" from the floor
Section needs70" of clear height, minimum
Top shelfNone, by design
Depth24"
Section width24" to 36"

Every one of these feeds the price. Change a number in the 3D builder and the parts list and the total change with it.

What the open top changes

A standard section is capped with a shelf at 84 inches. That shelf is useful, but it also means the top rod has to sit under it, and whatever goes on it is over your head at the back of a 24 inch deep shelf where you cannot see it.

Leave the shelf off and the section runs clear to the ceiling. The upper rod sets from the ceiling line instead, you get a few more inches of drop, and the whole wall reads as one clean line instead of a shelf band with stuff on it.

When to choose it

In a room with a 9 or 10 foot ceiling, where a top shelf would be unreachable anyway.

In a closet you want to look like cabinetry rather than storage. The open top is the single thing that makes a run look designed.

When you already have enough shelving elsewhere in the room, so the top band is not pulling its weight.

What you give up

The top shelf is real storage. On a 36 inch section at 24 deep, it is six square feet. Luggage, out of season bedding and bins live there in most closets.

It is also a dust ledge, which is the honest argument against it. An open top has nothing to dust.

You can mix. Open top over the hanging, capped shelf over the drawer tower next to it. The builder lets you set it per section, which is how it gets built on site anyway.

Before you ask

Questions about this layout

If yours is not here, the answer is a phone call away. Ask us directly.

Why would I leave the top shelf off?

Taller hanging, a cleaner line down the wall, and nothing stored over your head where you cannot see it. In a 9 or 10 foot room the top shelf is unreachable anyway.

Do I lose storage?

Yes. On a 36 inch section at 24 inches deep, the top shelf is about six square feet. That is where luggage and out of season bedding usually live.

Can I have an open top on some sections only?

Yes, it is set per section. Open over the hanging and capped over the drawers is a common mix.

Does it cost less without the shelf?

A little, because it is one less panel and less edgeband. The builder shows the difference as you toggle it.

Does an open top need a different ceiling?

No, but it reads best on a flat ceiling with no bulkhead. If there is a soffit over the closet we will say so at the measure.
