Pull-out shelves

A solid shelf on full-extension glides. It comes out to you, so the back of a deep run is as usable as the front and a folded stack never has to be lifted off.

Put this in a section, priced live Or book a free measure →

Where it goes

The bottom half only

It sits on the floor of the section, because it pulls or tilts out toward you and has to clear what is above it. The builder will not offer it in the top half.

The numbers

What this piece needs

Where it goesBottom half of a section only
Shelves per bankOne to six
Bank height12 to 60 inches
Usual spacingThe bank height divided by the number of shelves
GlidesFull extension undermount, soft close, a pair per shelf
ShelfThree-quarter-inch board, edge banded, same color as the closet

Every one of these changes what gets built. Change a number in the 3D builder and the drawing and the parts list change with it.

A shelf that comes to you

A fixed shelf makes you reach over whatever is on the front of it to get at whatever is behind. On a 24 inch run that is most of the shelf. A pull-out brings the whole shelf out past the face of the tower, so the back of it is as reachable as the front.

It is the right piece for folded stacks you do not want to lift: knitwear, jeans, towels. It is also the one that makes a deep closet work, and the one to use above shoulder height where you cannot see onto a fixed shelf at all.

The same glides a drawer runs on

Each shelf runs on a pair of full-extension soft-close undermount glides, ordered off the same table a drawer of that depth is ordered from. Nothing about a pull-out is a special part.

Full extension is the point of it. A half-extension runner leaves the back third of the shelf inside the tower, which is the thing a pull-out was meant to fix.

Why they are a bottom piece

Same as drawers and baskets. It pulls out toward you and you look down onto it, so the builder offers it in the bottom half of a section.

Over the bank you get a fixed shelf, and above that a rod or adjustable shelves, which is the usual make-up of a section with pull-outs in it.

Before you ask

Questions about this piece

If yours is not here, the answer is a phone call away. Ask us directly.

Pull-out shelves or drawers?

A drawer has sides, so it holds small things that would slide off. A pull-out shelf is open, so it takes a folded stack you want to lift off in one piece. Many closets have both in different sections.

How many pull-outs fit?

One to six in a bank, with the bank 12 to 60 inches tall. The spacing is the bank height divided by the number you pick.

Can pull-outs go at the top of a section?

Not in the builder. You look down onto a pull-out, so it is offered in the bottom half. If you want one high up for a reason, say so at the measure and we will draw it.

Are the glides full extension?

Yes, always, and soft close. They are the same undermount glides the drawers run on.

What is the shelf made of?

The same three-quarter-inch board as the rest of the closet, in the same color, with the exposed edge banded.
