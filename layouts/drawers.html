Drawers

A bank of soft-close drawers at the bottom of a section. The piece that stops a closet being only rails and shelves.

Where it goes

The bottom half only

It sits on the floor of the section, because it pulls or tilts out toward you and has to clear what is above it. The builder will not offer it in the top half.

Why drawers are a bottom piece

A drawer pulls out toward you. Above about chest height you cannot see into it, so you are reaching blind into a box over your head. That is why the builder will not offer drawers in the top half of a section.

Put them at the bottom and whatever goes above them, a rod or shelves, is at the height you actually use. A drawer bank with a rod over it is the most common section in a bedroom closet for exactly this reason.

What a drawer actually is

A box on glides with a front screwed to it. The box is a real stocked size, nine of them, from seven and a half inches tall to twenty five. The front is cut to whatever the section needs.

That is why a bank has to be designed around the stock. Four drawers across 34 inches gives eight and a half inch fronts, which suits the seven and a half inch box. Eight drawers in the same 34 inches gives four inch fronts, and nothing fits behind them.

The builder checks this as you change it and tells you when the numbers stop working, rather than drawing something that cannot be ordered.

Dividers matter more than drawers

A deep undivided drawer is a laundry basket with a front on it. What makes a drawer bank worth the money is what is inside: a jewellery insert, a watch tray, belt and tie rolls, a felt lined top drawer.

We usually divide the top one or two and leave the rest open. Those are the ones you open every day.

A bank is always capped with a fixed top shelf. It carries the upper glides and stops the bank racking, and it is a real part on the list rather than a drawing convention.

Before you ask

Questions about this piece

If yours is not here, the answer is a phone call away. Ask us directly.

Why can I not put drawers at the top?

Because you cannot see into a drawer above chest height. The builder offers drawers in the bottom half of a section only.

How many drawers can I have?

One to eight in a bank, with the bank anywhere from 10 to 48 inches tall. Four is the usual.

Why does the builder say no stock box fits?

The opening you drew is narrower or shorter than the smallest box we stock. Nine box sizes exist, and the drawing has to be buildable from them.

What glides do you use?

Full extension undermount soft close. You do not see the hardware, and the back of the drawer clears the carcass so the whole box is usable.

Can the fronts be a different color from the carcass?

Yes. They are picked separately and the builder has a tab for each.
