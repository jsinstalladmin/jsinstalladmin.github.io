Hanging over drawers

Four drawers at the bottom, a rod above them. The section most bedroom closets want two of.

The most useful section in the house

Drawers at the bottom, a rod above. Socks, underwear and folded trousers in the drawers, shirts and jackets on the rod, all inside one 30 inch section.

Most bedroom closets want two of these, one each side, with hanging or shelving between them. It is the layout that lets a couple share a closet without arguing about it.

Why the rod above is a short hang

A double hang needs 70 inches of its own. Put it over a 34 inch drawer bank and the section needs 104 inches, in a closet that has 80. It cannot be built, and any drawing that shows it is wrong.

So the rod above a drawer bank is a short hang: one rod with 38 to 54 inches of clear drop. That takes shirts, jackets and folded trousers, which is exactly what goes over a drawer bank anyway.

If you want a double hang, the drawers go in their own section beside it, not under it.

Heights that work

A 34 inch bank of four drawers leaves 46 inches of drop above it in a standard closet. That is a comfortable short hang.

A 28 inch bank of three deeper drawers leaves 52 inches, which starts to take a long jacket.

Past about 40 inches of drawers the rod above stops being useful, and you are better off with a full drawer tower and hanging somewhere else.

Before you ask

Questions about this layout

If yours is not here, the answer is a phone call away. Ask us directly.

Why can I not have a double hang over the drawers?

A double hang needs 70 inches and the bank takes 34, which is 104 inches in a closet that has 80. The rod above a drawer bank is a short hang, with 38 to 54 inches of drop.

How many drawers go under a rod?

Four at 34 inches is the standard. Three deeper drawers at 28 inches leaves more drop above.

Should I have two of these?

In a shared bedroom closet, usually yes, one each side. It is the layout that stops a couple fighting over the closet.

What goes in the drawers?

Socks, underwear, folded trousers, workout clothes. The top drawer is the one that benefits from dividers.

Is the drawer bank capped?

Always, with a fixed top shelf. It carries the upper glides and keeps the bank from racking.
