Adjustable shelves

Shelves that move. Drilled on 32mm centres the full height of the panel, so the spacing you pick today is not the spacing you are stuck with.

Where it goes

Either half of a section

It can fill a whole section on its own, go in the top half with something underneath it, or go underneath something else. The builder offers it in every one of those places.

Adjustable means drilled, not promised

Every vertical panel is drilled top to bottom at 32mm centres before it leaves the shop. The shelves sit on pins in those holes. Moving one is thirty seconds with your hands, and there is no new hole to drill and nothing to patch.

A shelf that is fixed in place is cheaper to build and it is the thing people regret first. Seasons change, a child grows, you buy taller boxes. The holes are already there.

The 36 inch span

A three quarter inch shelf sags under a normal load somewhere past 36 inches. That is not a style rule, it is the board. It is why sections are 24 to 36 inches wide and why a wider run gets a partition rather than a longer shelf.

If you want an unbroken look across a wide wall you get it from the shelves lining up across several sections, not from one shelf spanning the lot.

How many is the right number

Six is the usual for a full height section, which gives about 13 inches of clear space between each. Folded knits want 12 to 14. Boxes and baskets want whatever the box is plus two inches.

The builder draws what you ask for and tells you the clear opening each time you change the count, so you can check a real sweater against a real number instead of guessing.

Before you ask

Questions about this piece

If yours is not here, the answer is a phone call away. Ask us directly.

How many shelves fit in one section?

Up to ten. Six is the usual in a full height section and gives roughly 13 inches of clear space between each one.

Can I move them after it is installed?

Yes, that is the point. The panels are drilled the full height at 32mm centres, so a shelf lifts off its pins and goes back two holes up.

How deep are the shelves?

Fourteen inches for folded storage. If the section also hangs, the whole section is 24 inches deep and the shelves match it.

Why can I not have one shelf across the whole wall?

Past 36 inches a three quarter inch shelf bows. A wide wall is built as several sections with the shelves lined up, which looks the same and does not sag.

Are the edges finished?

Every exposed edge is banded, including the front edge of every shelf. Nothing is left raw to swell.
