Tilt-out hamper

A basket behind a front that tips out toward you. Laundry off the floor, out of sight, and in the closet rather than on the landing.

Put this in a section, priced live Or book a free measure →

Where it goes

The bottom half only

It sits on the floor of the section, because it pulls or tilts out toward you and has to clear what is above it. The builder will not offer it in the top half.

The numbers

What this piece needs

Where it goesBottom half of a section only
Height it takes26 to 32 inches off the floor
Basket18 inches, removable
FrontMatches the section, on a flap stay so it tips rather than swings
What fits aboveA rod, which becomes a short hang, or shelves
Section width24 to 36 inches

Every one of these feeds the price. Change a number in the 3D builder and the parts list and the total change with it.

Laundry belongs in the closet

The clothes come off in the closet. If the basket is on the landing, that is where the clothes go instead, and the floor of the closet becomes the basket.

A tilt-out hamper puts it where you undress, behind a front that matches the rest of the cabinetry, so the closet still looks finished with a week of washing in it.

It takes the bottom 28 inches

A hamper needs about 26 to 32 inches of height, and it has to be at the bottom because the front tips out and down toward the floor.

That changes what goes above it. A rod over a hamper is a short hang of about 40 inches of drop, which suits shirts and folded trousers. If you want a full length hang in that section, the hamper goes somewhere else.

The basket comes out

The basket lifts out, so you carry the basket to the machine rather than carrying armfuls. It is a real removable basket, not a bag stapled to the back of a door.

The front is on a flap stay so it stops where you let go of it rather than swinging all the way open into your shins.

Before you ask

Questions about this piece

If yours is not here, the answer is a phone call away. Ask us directly.

How much space does a hamper take?

Between 26 and 32 inches of the bottom of a section, with an 18 inch basket inside it.

What can go above a hamper?

A rod, which becomes a short hang of about 40 inches, or shelves. Not a full length hang, there is not enough height left.

Does the basket come out?

Yes, it lifts out so you can carry it to the machine.

Does the front match the rest of the closet?

Yes, it is cut from the same board and finished the same way, so it reads as another drawer front.

Can I have two hampers?

Yes, in two sections, which is what people with a color sorting habit ask for.
