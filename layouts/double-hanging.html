Double hanging

Two rods stacked in one section, at 80 inches and 40 inches. Shirts and jackets up top, folded trousers below.

Why two rods beat one

A single rod at 66 inches leaves roughly four feet of dead air under every shirt. Split that section in two and the same wall holds twice as much, which is why double hanging is the default in almost every bedroom closet we build.

The upper rod sits at 80 inches and the lower at 40. That gives about 34 inches of clear drop up top and 32 below. A folded trouser on a clamp hanger is 30 inches. A shirt is 38 inches on the hanger but folds at the hem, so 34 works.

The section needs 70 inches of clear height for both rods to fit properly. In a standard 96 inch room with a top shelf at 84, there is 80 inches to play with, so a double hang fits with room to spare.

What does not go in it

Dresses, coats, gowns and robes. Anything that falls past the knee hits the lower rod. Those belong in a long hanging section, and most closets want one of each.

If you only have one wall and you wear a lot of dresses, run double hanging across most of it and give one full-height section to the long stuff. That single decision is what separates a closet that works from one that technically holds everything.

How we build it

The divider shelf between the two rods is a fixed shelf, not an adjustable one. It carries the lower rod and stops the partitions racking, so it is screwed, not pinned.

The rods are 1 inch oval steel on end sockets, set 12 inches off the back of a 24 inch deep section so a hanger hook sits square and the shoulder of a jacket does not rub the back panel.

Both partitions are full height and edge banded on the front edge. Past 36 inches of span the shelf over the rod will deflect, so wider openings get a partition rather than a longer shelf.

Before you ask

Questions about this layout

If yours is not here, the answer is a phone call away. Ask us directly.

How much height does a double hang need?

Seventy inches of clear height in the section. In a standard 96 inch room with the top shelf at 84, you have 80 inches, so it fits with room over it.

Can I put a double hang over a drawer bank?

No. The two together need about 104 inches and a standard closet has 80. The builder will not draw it. Over drawers you want a short hang instead.

What heights are the rods?

Eighty inches and forty inches from the floor, with a fixed divider shelf between them carrying the lower rod.

Will shirts fit under the upper rod?

Yes. There is about 34 inches of clear drop up top. A shirt on a hanger is 38 inches but folds at the hem.

Can I move the rods later?

The divider shelf between them is fixed, so no, not without a visit. That is deliberate: it carries the lower rod and keeps the section square.
