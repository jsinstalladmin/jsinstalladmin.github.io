Long hanging

One rod at 66 inches with clear floor under it. Dresses, coats, robes, anything long.

The one measurement that matters

A long hanging section is 62 inches of clear height, minimum. Below that a dress hem sits on the floor, and a floor length gown needs more than that again.

We set the rod at 66 inches, which gives about 52 inches of clear drop. That takes almost every dress, most coats and all robes. A wedding dress or a full length gown needs its own section, usually 72 inches of drop, and we will tell you at the measure.

How much of it you need

Most people need one section, not a wall. Count what you own that is longer than a jacket. If it is under thirty garments, one 24 to 36 inch section holds it.

The rest of the wall is better spent on double hanging, which holds twice as much per foot. Long hanging is the expensive storage in a closet, measured by what it holds for the wall it eats.

How we build it

One rod, end sockets, nothing under it. The floor stays clear, which is deliberate: it is the only place in a closet where a tall boot or a vacuum actually fits.

A shelf caps it at 84 inches as standard. If you would rather it ran open to the ceiling, we build it that way.

Depth is 24 inches. A coat on a wide wooden hanger is 22 inches across the shoulder, so a shallower section rubs the back and creases the shoulder.

Before you ask

Questions about this layout

If yours is not here, the answer is a phone call away. Ask us directly.

How tall does a long hanging section need to be?

Sixty two inches of clear height is the minimum. We set the rod at 66 inches, which gives about 52 inches of drop.

Will a floor length gown fit?

Usually not in a standard section. A full length gown wants about 72 inches of drop, which means its own taller section. Tell us at the measure and we will build one.

How many long hanging sections do I need?

Count what you own that is longer than a jacket. Under thirty garments, one section is enough. The rest of the wall does more work as double hanging.

Can I store things on the floor under it?

Yes, and that is one of its advantages. It is the only part of the closet where a tall boot or a vacuum actually fits.

Can it run open to the ceiling?

Yes. A shelf caps it at 84 inches as standard, but we build it open if you prefer.
