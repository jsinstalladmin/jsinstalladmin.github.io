Drawer tower

A bank of soft-close drawers with open shelving over it. The piece that stops a closet being only rails.

What a drawer actually is

A drawer is a box on glides with a front screwed to it. The box is a real stocked size, nine of them, from seven and a half inches tall to twenty five. The front is cut to whatever the section needs.

That is why a drawer bank has to be designed around the stock: four drawers across 34 inches gives eight and a half inch fronts, which suits the seven and a half inch box. Eight drawers in the same 34 inches would give four inch fronts, and nothing fits behind them.

The builder checks this as you change it and tells you when the numbers stop working, rather than drawing something that cannot be ordered.

Glides and soft close

Undermount, full extension, soft close. Undermount means you do not see the hardware when the drawer is open. Full extension means the back of the drawer clears the carcass, so the back third is usable.

Side mount glides are cheaper and you can see them. We do not fit them in a closet you are paying for.

Dividers matter more than drawers

A deep undivided drawer is a laundry basket with a front on it. The thing that makes a drawer tower worth the money is what is inside it: a jewellery insert, a watch tray, belt and tie rolls, a felt lined top drawer.

We usually divide the top one or two and leave the rest open. Those are the ones people open every day.

A drawer bank is always capped with a fixed top shelf. It carries the upper glides and stops the bank racking, and it is a real part on the parts list rather than a drawing convention.

Before you ask

Questions about this layout

If yours is not here, the answer is a phone call away. Ask us directly.

How many drawers can I have?

One to eight in a bank, with the bank anywhere from 10 to 48 inches tall. Four is the usual.

Why does the builder say no stock box fits?

Because the opening you drew is narrower or shorter than the smallest box we stock. Nine box sizes exist, from seven and a half inches tall to twenty five, and the drawing has to be buildable from them.

What glides do you use?

Full extension undermount soft close. You do not see the hardware, and the back of the drawer clears the carcass so the whole box is usable.

Can the drawer fronts be a different finish?

Yes. The carcass and the fronts are picked separately, and the builder has a tab for each.

Are dividers extra?

They are itemised, so you see what each one costs. We usually divide the top one or two drawers and leave the rest open.
