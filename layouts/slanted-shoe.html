Slanted shoe shelves

Angled shelves with a rail along the front. The shoe tips toward you, so you see the whole thing rather than its toe.

Where it goes

Either half of a section

It can fill a whole section on its own, go in the top half with something underneath it, or go underneath something else. The builder offers it in every one of those places.

What the angle buys you

A shoe on a flat shelf shows you its toe. A shoe on a shelf pitched 15 degrees toward you shows you the whole upper, the color and the heel. Standing in the doorway you can see every pair at once instead of crouching and pulling things out.

That is worth doing for the shoes you choose between. It is not worth doing for the ones you own two of and wear every day.

The rail is not decoration

A pitched shelf needs a rail along its front edge or the shoe walks off it, slowly, over weeks. The rail is a real part on the parts list.

It also sets what will fit. A heel hooks over it and sits nicely. A boot will not, which is why tall things belong on flat shelves or in an open tower.

How much of your closet should be slanted

A section or two. Slanted shelves hold fewer pairs per foot than flat ones, because the shoe takes more vertical space when it is tilted, and they take nothing but shoes.

The usual answer is one slanted section at eye level for the collection, flat shelves at the bottom for the everyday pairs, and an open tower for boots.

Before you ask

Questions about this piece

If yours is not here, the answer is a phone call away. Ask us directly.

How many pairs per shelf?

About three in a 30 inch section, and the spacing is 8 inches, same as flat shoe shelving.

Do slanted shelves hold less than flat ones?

Yes, a little, because a tilted shoe is taller. You trade a few pairs for being able to see them all.

Will boots fit on a slanted shelf?

No. Put boots on flat shelves or leave a section open for them.

Is there a rail along the front?

Yes, on every slanted shelf, and it is itemised on the parts list. Without it the shoes slide off.

Can I mix slanted and flat in one closet?

That is what most people do. One slanted section for the shoes you choose between, flat shelves for the rest.
