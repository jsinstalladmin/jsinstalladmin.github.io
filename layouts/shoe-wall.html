Shoe wall

Angled shelves with a front fence, the full height of the section. You see the shoe, not the toe.

The numbers

What this section needs

Shelf angleAbout 10 degrees, with a fence on the front edge
SpacingRoughly 10" between angled shelves, 8" if they are flat
Pairs per footTwo on a 12" shelf, more for flats, fewer for boots
BootsA flat shelf with 16" to 18" over it, not an angled one
Depth12" to 14"
Section height range16" to 84"

Every one of these feeds the price. Change a number in the 3D builder and the parts list and the total change with it.

Angled beats flat, for shoes you wear

A flat shelf shows you a row of toes. An angled shelf with a fence on the front edge shows the whole shoe, so you pick the pair you want instead of pulling three out to look at them.

Angled also fits more pairs per foot, because the heels nest into the gap behind the pair in front.

What it does not suit

Boots. A knee boot needs a flat shelf with 16 to 18 inches over it, or a boot bar, not an angled shelf.

Trainers with a thick sole sit high on an angled shelf and eat the spacing. If most of your collection is trainers, flat shelves at 8 inches hold more.

Most shoe walls are a mix: angled for heels and flats, a couple of flat shelves at the bottom for boots and anything bulky.

Numbers

Roughly two pairs per foot of shelf, so a 30 inch section holds about five pairs per shelf.

Angled shelves are spaced about 10 inches apart, flat ones about 8.

Depth is 12 to 14 inches. A mens size 12 is 12 and a half inches long, which is why 12 inches is the floor and not the target.

Before you ask

Questions about this layout

If yours is not here, the answer is a phone call away. Ask us directly.

How many pairs will it hold?

About two pairs per foot of shelf, so roughly five pairs per shelf in a 30 inch section. Flats hold more, boots far fewer.

Angled or flat?

Angled for heels and flats, because you see the whole shoe and the heels nest. Flat for trainers and boots. Most shoe walls are a mix.

How far apart are the shelves?

About 10 inches for angled shelves, 8 for flat ones, and 16 to 18 for a boot shelf.

How deep?

Twelve to fourteen inches. A mens size 12 is twelve and a half inches long, so twelve is the floor rather than the target.

Do the shelves have a fence?

The angled ones do, on the front edge. That is what holds the shoe on the slope.
