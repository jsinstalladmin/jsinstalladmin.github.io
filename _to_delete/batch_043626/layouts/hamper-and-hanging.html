Tilt-out hamper with hanging over it

A tilt-down basket at the bottom, a rod above it. The pile on the floor gets somewhere to go.

The pile on the floor

Every closet has one. A tilt out hamper is the cheapest thing on this site that changes how a room feels day to day, because the laundry has somewhere to be that is not the floor.

The front tilts down and out from its bottom edge, so the basket comes to you. There is no door to open and nothing to lift out.

Why the rod above is short

A hamper takes the bottom 28 inches of the section. In a standard closet that leaves 52 inches over it, and a long hang needs 62. So the rod above a hamper is a short hang, with 38 to 54 inches of drop.

That is not a compromise, it is the product. Shirts, jackets and folded trousers are exactly what goes over a hamper.

What cannot go in front of it

Nothing. The front swings down and out through the space in front of the section, so a pull out basket, a drawer or a counter in that same section will collide with it.

The builder blocks those combinations rather than letting you draw something that jams on day one. It also stops you putting a hamper where a door swing or a corner would trap it.

In a corner, the hamper goes in the section away from the return, for the same reason.

Before you ask

Questions about this layout

If yours is not here, the answer is a phone call away. Ask us directly.

How tall is the hamper?

Twenty six to thirty two inches. Twenty eight is what we draw, with an 18 inch chrome basket inside.

Why is the rod above it short?

The hamper takes the bottom 28 inches, which leaves 52 over it. A long hang needs 62 and a double hang needs 70, so the rod above a hamper is a short hang with 38 to 54 inches of drop.

Can I put a drawer next to it?

In the next section, yes. In the same section, no, because the front tilts down and out through that space. The builder blocks the combination.

Does it work in a corner?

It goes in the section away from the return, so the tilting front is not trapped by the adjacent run.

Can the hamper front match the drawer fronts?

Yes. It takes the fronts finish and the same pull as everything else on the run.
