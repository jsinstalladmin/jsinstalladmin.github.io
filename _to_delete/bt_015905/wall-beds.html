Wall Beds

A wall bed turns a room you use twice a year back into a room you use every day.

Queen vertical · Wall 68" wide, 88" tallQueen horizontal · Wall 88" wide, 68" tallDouble vertical · Wall 60" wide, 88" tall

Built to the room,
not to a catalogue

We build wall beds in six configurations: single, double and queen, each vertical or horizontal, with the surrounding cabinetry built to the same wall so the whole thing reads as one piece of joinery rather than a bed bolted to a wall.

Horizontal suits a room with a low ceiling or a window you do not want to lose. Vertical suits a narrow wall. The mechanism is a counterbalanced lift rated for daily use; the bed stays made when it folds up.

Questions

Does it stay made when it folds up?
Yes. There is a retaining strap across the mattress.
What mattress does it take?
A standard mattress up to roughly 12 inches thick. No special mattress required.
Does it have to be fixed to the wall?
Yes, into studs. That is a safety requirement, not a preference.
Can you build cabinets around it?
Yes, and that is usually the point: towers either side and a bridge over the top, all in the same finish.
