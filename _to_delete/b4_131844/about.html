How we work

JS Installations & Closets is a closet installation company working across the East Coast. We measure, draw, order and install, all of it ourselves.

That matters more than it sounds. The most common reason a closet install goes wrong is that the person who took the measurements is not the person holding the drill, and nobody notices the baseboard is three-quarters of an inch proud until the panel is already cut. When the same people do both, that conversation happens at the measure instead of on install day.

We order our components through ClosetPro from a wholesale manufacturer, which means the parts are cut to our dimensions on an industrial line rather than trimmed on site. What arrives is already right. The install is assembly, not carpentry.

What we will tell you honestly

When a system is not worth itA rental, a closet you are about to move out of, or a room that needs a builder before it needs us.
When your room is not a walk-inUnder five feet wide, it is a reach-in. We will build you a very good reach-in.
Where lighting can and cannot goLight strips fit fixed shelves. Adjustable shelves move, so they cannot carry a wired strip.
Why a shelf will not span eight feetThree-quarter-inch melamine sags past thirty-six inches. We put a partition in instead.
