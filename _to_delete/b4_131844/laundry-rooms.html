Laundry Rooms

A laundry room needs three things the builder never gives it: somewhere to fold, somewhere to hang, and somewhere for the baskets to live.

Front-load counter · 36"–38" above the floorTop-load · No counterHanging rod · Minimum 48" clear below

Built to the room,
not to a catalogue

The counter is the piece that changes the room. A surface across the top of a front-loading pair turns the whole wall into a folding station, and it is the first thing people say they would have done years earlier.

After that it is a short rod for anything that comes out of the dryer on a hanger, a tilt-out hamper so the baskets are off the floor, and shelving sized to detergent bottles rather than to nothing in particular.

Questions

Can you build a counter over a top-loader?
No. You need the lids to open. We put shelving above instead, 18 inches clear over the lid.
Will the counter take the weight of a full basket?
Yes. Counters are carried on partitions and a cleat into studs.
Can you include the hamper?
Yes. The tilt-out hamper is a standard part with its own bracket and flap stay, in three basket sizes.
