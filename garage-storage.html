Garage Storage

A garage gets taken over from the floor up. The fix is to get everything onto the wall and leave the slab clear.

Over the hood · 84" and upTall lockers · Full height, 24" deepWorkbench · 36" counter, 24" deep

Built to the room,
not to a catalogue

Garage storage has one rule the inside of the house does not: get it off the concrete. Florida slabs sweat, and anything cardboard sitting on one for a summer is finished. Every garage system we build is wall-hung or sits on a sealed toe kick.

The other difference is depth. Garage shelving carries awkward, heavy things, so runs go deeper, 16 to 24 inches, and sections get narrower to keep the shelves from sagging under the load.

Questions

Will it hold tools and paint?
Yes. The load limit is shelf span, not the material, which is why garage sections run narrower than closet sections.
Can it be wall-hung so I can hose the floor?
Yes, and in Florida that is what we recommend.
Do you build around the water heater and panel?
Yes. Filler panels and partial-height panels exist for exactly that.
