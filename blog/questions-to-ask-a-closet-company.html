12 Questions to Ask Before You Sign With a Closet Company

Most of these take ten seconds to ask and will tell you more than any showroom.

6 min readUpdated 2026-01-28

On who actually does the work

  1. Does the person who measures my room also draw it? If the answer is no, something gets lost between the two.
  2. Do your installers work for you, or are they subcontracted? Subcontracted crews are paid by the job, which rewards speed over care.
  3. Will the same crew that installs it come back if something is wrong?

On the quote

  1. Is this price installed, or is installation separate?
  2. Does it include removing the existing shelving?
  3. Does it include patching the holes you drill?
  4. What happens to the price if my walls are out of square? Every house is. The answer should be nothing.
  5. Is there a deposit, and when is it due? Ours is after you have seen a drawing and a fixed price, not before.

On the materials

  1. What is the core material and how thick is it? Three-quarter-inch is the answer you want. Half-inch sags.
  2. Is every exposed edge banded? Unbanded edges swell the first time they get wet.
  3. What is the lead time, and does it change by finish? Gloss finishes genuinely take longer. A company that quotes the same lead time for everything has not checked.

The one that matters most

Ask them what they would not do in your closet. A designer who only agrees with you is selling. A designer who tells you a wired LED strip cannot go on an adjustable shelf, or that a shelf past 36 inches will bow, is building.

We will tell you that before you pay for it, which occasionally costs us a sale and has never once cost us a callback.
