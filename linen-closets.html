Linen Closets

Linen closets are the easiest room in the house to get right and the one builders most often get wrong.

Bath towels · 12" spacing, 14" deepHand towels & washcloths · 8" spacing, 12" deepSheet sets · 10" spacing, 14" deep

Built to the room,
not to a catalogue

A folded bath towel is about 12 inches of stack. A set of sheets is about 8. Builder's linen shelves are usually spaced at 16 inches and 16 inches deep, which means every stack has air above it and a second row behind it that you forget exists.

Shelves at 12 to 14 inches deep, spaced to the stack rather than to a round number, with a couple of deep drawers at the base for spare duvets, is the whole job.

Questions

How deep should a linen shelf be?
Twelve to fourteen inches. Deeper puts a second row behind the first, which is where towels go to be forgotten.
Can you fit it into a hallway closet with a door?
Yes. We build inside the opening and the existing door still closes.
