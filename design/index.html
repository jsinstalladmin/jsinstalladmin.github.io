About this estimate

Is this the real price?
It is built from the same parts list and the same per-square-foot finish costs we order from, so it is far closer than a range on a brochure. What it cannot know is your walls: baseboard, out-of-square corners, an air return in the way. The measure settles those and the figure moves a little either way.
Does it include installation?
No. The figure is the cabinetry itself. Installation is quoted after we measure, because it depends on access, the shelving that has to come out and whether the baseboard has to come off.
Why can't a bay be wider than 36 inches?
Because three-quarter-inch melamine sags past that under load. The builder will warn you rather than draw something we would not fit.
What is the order list for?
It is every panel, shelf, rod, drawer box, glide, basket and pull your design needs, with sizes and counts. Send it with your enquiry and we are already most of the way to a quote.
My room isn't a rectangle.
Build the closest rectangle to get the number, then tell us in the notes. Angled ceilings, bulkheads and returns are normal and there are parts for all of them.
