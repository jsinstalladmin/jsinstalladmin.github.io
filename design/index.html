About this design

When do I get a price?
Within 24 hours. Joseph prices every closet himself, against your real room and your real access, so he works out the figure on the design you send and comes back inside a day. No deposit and no obligation to get it.
Why is there no price on the screen?
Because a number here would be a guess, and you would have to un-learn it the moment we measured. What is on the screen instead is the thing worth checking: every piece that goes into your closet, down to the shelf pins. Get that right and the price follows.
Does it include installation?
The price Joseph sends covers the cabinetry and the fitting, settled together once we know the access, what has to come out and whether the baseboard comes off.
Why can’t a tower be wider than 36 inches?
A three-quarter inch shelf sags under a normal load past about 36 inches. Wider than that and a partition goes in instead, which is why those are the only widths offered.
What is the piece list for?
It is what gets built. Every panel, shelf, rod, drawer box, glide, basket, hinge, cam and screw in your design is on it, and it goes to Joseph with your drawing so nothing is guessed at afterwards.
My room isn’t a rectangle.
Build the walls you do have and say so in the message. The measure sorts out the rest, and it is free.
