Finishes

Seventy-eight of them, across four price levels. Pick one for the carcass and a different one for the drawer fronts if you want. Most of the best-looking closets we build do exactly that.

Filter

What the level means

A level is a price band, not a quality grade

Every one of these is the same ¾" thermally fused laminate on the same furniture-grade core, edge banded the same way. The level is what the board costs per square foot, which is set by how the decor is printed and how the surface is finished. A Level 5 closet is not built better than a Level 2 one. It costs more because the board does.

Level 1Stock white. One week, the cheapest board on the list, and the one most closets are built from.1 finish
Level 2The everyday range: woodgrains, solids and textiles. Two to four weeks. Roughly twice the board cost of white.61 finishes
Level 3Deeper textures and synchronised grains, where the surface you feel lines up with the grain you see.11 finishes

Level 5High gloss and premium panels. Two to eight weeks depending on the decor, and four to six times the board cost of white.5 finishes

The lead time on each swatch is the real one for that board, not an average. If you are in a hurry, filter to Level 1 and 2 and look at the one-week options.

See it on a real closet

A 120-pixel square tells you the colour and nothing about how a finish behaves across a whole wall: how the grain runs, how it takes the light, what it does next to a drawer front in a different tone.

The builder renders your finish choice onto a full closet so you can see it at size before you commit, and we bring the physical samples to the measure.

About the material

What is the material?
Thermally fused laminate over a three-quarter-inch engineered core. The decorative layer is fused to the board under heat and pressure rather than glued on, so it does not peel at the edge the way a laminate veneer can. Every exposed edge is banded in a matching tape.
Do the price levels mean a difference in quality?
No. The core and the construction are identical across all four levels. The level reflects what the decorative surface costs: a standard woodgrain, a textured synchronised grain, a matte, or a high-gloss. A Level 2 finish is not a cheaper closet, it is a cheaper surface.
How long do they take?
Most of the Level 2 and 3 finishes are two to three weeks. The high-gloss lines run five to eight weeks because they are made to order.
Can I mix finishes in one closet?
Yes, and it is one of the better things you can do with the budget: carcass in a quieter finish and drawer fronts in something with more character. The builder lets you set them separately.
Will the finish match my floor or my kitchen?
Bring us a sample or a photo and we will put the nearest three in front of you. Matching a floor exactly is usually a mistake. A close-but-not-exact match reads as a mistake, where a deliberate contrast reads as a choice.
Do you have physical samples?
Yes. We bring the shortlist to the measure appointment, because a finish on a screen and a finish in your room's light are different things.
