About the material

What is the material?
Thermally fused laminate over a three-quarter-inch engineered core. The decorative layer is fused to the board under heat and pressure rather than glued on, so it does not peel at the edge the way a laminate veneer can. Every exposed edge is banded in a matching tape.
Do the price levels mean a difference in quality?
No. The core and the construction are identical across all four levels. The level reflects what the decorative surface costs — a standard woodgrain, a textured synchronised grain, a matte, or a high-gloss. A Level 2 finish is not a cheaper closet, it is a cheaper surface.
How long do they take?
Most of the Level 2 and 3 finishes are two to three weeks. The high-gloss lines run five to eight weeks because they are made to order.
Can I mix finishes in one closet?
Yes, and it is one of the better things you can do with the budget — carcass in a quieter finish and drawer fronts in something with more character. The builder lets you set them separately.
Will the finish match my floor or my kitchen?
Bring us a sample or a photo and we will put the nearest three in front of you. Matching a floor exactly is usually a mistake — a close-but-not-exact match reads as a mistake, where a deliberate contrast reads as a choice.
Do you have physical samples?
Yes. We bring the shortlist to the measure appointment, because a finish on a screen and a finish in your room's light are different things.
